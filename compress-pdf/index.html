How to compress a PDF

  1. Add your PDF — drag it into the box above, or click to browse.
  2. Choose a preset — "Smallest file" for the tiniest result, "Balanced" for everyday use, or "Best quality" when sharpness matters. You can fine-tune with the quality slider.
  3. Click "Compress PDF" — each page is rebuilt as an image, on your device.
  4. Check the report — you will see the old size, the new size, and how much you saved, then download the file.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my PDF barely shrink?

Compression works by turning pages into images. If your PDF is mostly text, the original file is already small and efficient — turning its pages into images can even make it bigger. The tool works best on scanned documents and PDFs full of photos.

Can I still select or search text after compression?

No. After compression, each page is an image, so text can no longer be selected, copied or searched. Keep your original file if you need the text later.

Which preset should I choose?

For forms and email attachments, "Balanced" is usually right. Use "Smallest file" when a form has a strict size limit, and "Best quality" when the PDF will be printed or read closely.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser, on your device. Your files are never sent anywhere.
