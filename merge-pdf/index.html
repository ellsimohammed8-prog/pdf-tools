    How to merge PDFs

    1. Add your PDFs — drag them into the box above, or click to browse. Add as many as you like.
    2. Arrange the order — use the up and down arrows to put the files in the order you want them to appear in the final document. Remove any file with the × button.
    3. Click "Merge PDFs" — the files are combined on your device, in seconds.
    4. Download — the merged PDF downloads straight to your computer or phone.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    As many as you like. There is no limit on this site — the only practical limit is your device's memory. Merging dozens of files works fine on a normal computer.

    Can I change the order of the files?

    Yes. Use the up and down arrows next to each file to move it. The merged PDF follows the order shown in the list, from top to bottom.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Merging happens entirely in your browser, on your device. Your files are never sent anywhere, and they are never stored.

    Does merging change the quality of my PDFs?

    No. Merging only rearranges pages — the content of each page is copied exactly as it was. Text, images and formatting stay the same.
