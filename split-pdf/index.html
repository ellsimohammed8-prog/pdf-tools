How to split a PDF

  1. Add your PDF — drag it into the box above, or click to browse.
  2. Choose a mode — extract specific pages ("1-3, 5"), split every few pages, or take every page separately.
  3. Click "Split PDF" — the new file (or ZIP of files) downloads straight to your device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write the pages I want?

Use page numbers with commas and dashes, for example "1-3, 5, 7-9" means pages 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8 and 9. Page 1 is the first page of the document.

What do I get when I split into many parts?

You get one PDF per part, packed together in a single ZIP file so you only download once. Unzip it to get the individual files.

Does splitting change the pages?

No. Splitting only copies pages — the text, images and formatting of each page stay exactly as they were.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Splitting happens entirely in your browser, on your device. Your files are never sent anywhere.
